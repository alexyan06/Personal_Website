---PROMPT---
Draw a street-level view of the small town shown in the first attached image, as one scene of an original 8-bit NES-era role-playing game. Match the second attached image exactly in style: it is an approved room from the same game. Same chunky pixel scale, same flat NES-style colors, same dark outlines, same top-down three-quarter overhead view. Genuine low-resolution pixel art: imagine a 320x180 pixel image scaled up so every art pixel is a crisp square block. Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, gradients, blur or soft lighting.

The four buildings from the first image stand side by side across the upper half of the scene, each about a quarter of the width, and each COMPLETELY visible from its roof down to its door, with nothing cut off at the top. In this order from left to right:
1. A market stall with a green-and-white striped awning and crates of produce on its counter.
2. A white stone bank with columns, a triangular roof and wide front steps.
3. A red brick hall with a clock tower on top.
4. A small grey garage with its roll-up door half open, a workbench visible inside.
Above each building's door hangs a plain blank wooden sign board with nothing written on it.
The lower half is an open grey cobblestone street seen from above (individual rounded cobbles, clearly a street, not a wall), with two old street lamps, a bench and a small planter. Keep it calm and open. The street continues out of the scene at the bottom center.

BACKGROUND: everything outside the scene itself (any empty area around or beyond the room, and any sky) must be one single, completely flat, solid magenta color, hex #FF00FF, with a clean hard edge against the scene. No black, no gradient, no texture, no shadow in that area. This magenta is removed afterward, so any variation in it shows up as a defect. The sky above the rooftops is part of that magenta background.

Leave a strip of empty floor or wall directly below each listed object so a text label can be placed there later. Bottom center: an open doorway leading out.

No people or characters. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, words, logos or UI anywhere in the image; any sign or label surface is left plain and blank.
