---PROMPT---
Draw the inside of the stone library shown in the first attached image, as one room of an original 8-bit NES-era role-playing game. Match the second attached image exactly in style: it is an approved room from the same game. Same chunky pixel scale, same flat NES-style colors, same dark outlines, same top-down three-quarter overhead view. Genuine low-resolution pixel art: imagine a 320x180 pixel image scaled up so every art pixel is a crisp square block. Hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing, gradients, blur or soft lighting.

A quiet library with grey stone walls and a grey stone floor. Tall wooden bookshelves full of colorful book spines line the back wall on both sides. In the middle of the back wall, a tall arched window with bright blue sky and white frame, letting a beam of light fall onto the floor (drawn as a flat lighter patch, not a gradient). A lit candle on a small stand beside the window.

Exactly four small wooden reading desks stand in one row across the middle of the room, evenly spaced:
- First desk: a large open book with a red cover.
- Second desk: a large open book with a light-blue cover.
- Third and fourth desks: empty, just a small inkwell on each.
Against the right wall: a small separate wooden stand holding one thick closed book standing face-out, with a red bookmark ribbon hanging from it.
Nothing else on the floor: no rugs, mats or benches.

BACKGROUND: everything outside the scene itself (any empty area around or beyond the room, and any sky) must be one single, completely flat, solid magenta color, hex #FF00FF, with a clean hard edge against the scene. No black, no gradient, no texture, no shadow in that area. This magenta is removed afterward, so any variation in it shows up as a defect.

Leave a strip of empty floor or wall directly below each listed object so a text label can be placed there later. Bottom center: an open doorway leading out.

No people or characters. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, words, logos or UI anywhere in the image; any sign or label surface is left plain and blank.
